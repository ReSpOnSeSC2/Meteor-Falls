## Session log (chronological)

### Title / New Game / Name entry
- ✅ Title screen: "METEOR FALLS" logo + meteor streak + twinkling stars looks great. Menu (New Game / Sprite Lab) reads cleanly. Subtitle properly centered.
- ✅ Name-entry flow: 5 heroes prefilled with canon names (Jay, Mia, Milo, Pippa, Dorin), good prompts ("Name the girl who prays.", etc.). START accepts prefill. Letter grid (A pick / B erase / RANDOM / OK) works. Player/food/thing screens work.
- ✅ Recap screen reads correctly and the opaque window covers the grid.
- ❓/💡 (polish, low) Recap screen: the *previous* entry screen's prompt ("Name your coolest thing. The game will…") and its value box remain visible at the very top edge above the recap window. Hide the prior prompt/value while the recap is shown.

### 2 AM opening cinematic
- ✅ The meteor cinema is excellent: sleeping Otterbrook, Hickory Hill, crescent moon, growing meteor, impact whiteout, town porch-lights waking, push-in on Jay's house. Captions auto-advance and are well written ("One of the stars is getting bigger. Stars are not supposed to do that.").
- ✅ Bedroom wake-up dialogue (typewriter) is strong: "…Hickory Hill glows like a stove burner someone left on." Ends with a clear, explicit goal: "* GOAL: Go downstairs and check on Mom."

### Jay's Room (rex_bedroom)
- 💡 (notable) The player's OWN bedroom has **no examinable objects at all** (`signs: []`). Confirmed in-game: facing the desk + A does nothing. The bed, desk, and dresser give no flavor text. This is the player's very first moment of agency and currently rewards curiosity with silence. EarthBound's opening room rewards every examine. Recommend adding 2-3 flavor signs — e.g. the desk/computer riffing on the player's chosen "coolest thing" ({thing}); the baseball bat "leaning by the desk" the intro explicitly mentioned; the dresser/closet; maybe the bed offering a nap line. Big bang-for-buck on first impression.
